Quick guide

The essentials for managing your business with Sellbook. Choose a topic to open the relevant page.

1

Add your products

Open Products and tap +. Enter the product name, category, cost price, selling price and starting stock.

2

Record a sale

Open Sales, choose the products and quantities sold, review the total and save the sale.

3

Record expenses

Open Expenses and add what you paid for, such as transport, rent or supplies. Expenses are included in net profit.

4

Manage your stock

Use Stock management to review quantities and stock history. Restock products when supplies arrive, and check Home for low-stock alerts.

5

Understand your profit

Tap Net profit on Home to see daily profit history. Open Reports for a wider summary of sales, gross profit and expenses.

6

Manage Buybook customers

Open Reservations to review and respond to customer reservations. Use Subscribers to view and message people subscribed to your shop. Open Messages to continue your conversations.

7

Keep your records safe

Use Settings → Sync & backup to check cloud sync and create backups. Keep a recent backup, especially when changing phones.

Privacy policy Effective 23 October 2026

1. Information Sellbook handles

Sellbook may handle information needed to operate your account and business records, including:

  • Account details such as your email address and authentication identifier.
  • Products, prices, stock quantities, sales, expenses, notes and stock adjustments you enter.
  • Optional business profile details and product or business-logo images you choose to upload.
  • Records stored on your device, cached data and pending changes used for offline access and synchronisation.

2. How information is stored

Some records may be saved in the browser on your device. When you sign in and use cloud features, records and uploaded images are stored through Sellbook’s configured Supabase services. Google sign-in, when used, is handled by the relevant authentication provider.

Offline changes may remain on your device until you reconnect and synchronisation succeeds. Anyone who can access your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view locally stored records.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, sales and expense recording, stock management, summaries and reports, syncing, image display, backup and restore, and account-related functions. Avoid putting passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive personal information in business notes.

4. Access and sharing

Cloud records are intended to be separated by account. Sellbook relies on hosting, database and authentication providers to operate, and their own privacy terms may also apply. Do not share your login credentials or backup files with people you do not trust.

5. Backups, retention and deletion

Use the app’s backup/export feature to keep a copy of important records. Exported files may contain sensitive business information, so store them securely. Before deleting an account, export anything you need and resolve pending offline changes. Account deletion may permanently remove account data; it cannot necessarily remove copies already exported or held in independent backups.

6. Security and your choices

Use a strong, unique sign-in credential, keep your device locked and updated, sign out on shared devices, and make regular backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Only enter information needed to manage your business records.

7. Changes and privacy questions

This policy may be updated as Sellbook changes. Please use the Support option in the app to contact Sellbook about questions or concerns. Applicable privacy rights under Nigerian law are not intended to be excluded by this notice.

This policy explains the app’s intended data handling in plain language and is not legal advice.

Offline changes may stay on your device until connectivity returns and synchronisation succeeds. Network problems, storage limitations, conflicts or service interruptions can delay syncing. Check sync status before assuming another device has the latest information, and back up records regularly.
