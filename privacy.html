Privacy Notice

Sellbook · Made Business Simple.

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice explains what Sellbook collects, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to users. It applies to the Sellbook business-management service and the optional Buybook shop, subscription, messaging and reservation features connected to it.

Operator information required before unrestricted public launch

The service operator must publish its full legal name, residence or place of business, and a monitored privacy contact before Sellbook is opened to unrestricted public registration. These details have not been provided for this release, so they are intentionally not invented here. This notice is not complete for unrestricted launch until the operator fills them in.

Data controller/legal operator: To be supplied by the operator.

Residence/place of business: To be supplied by the operator.

Privacy and data-rights contact: A monitored email or other direct contact method must be supplied by the operator.

Information Sellbook handles

  • Account and authentication: email address, authentication identifier, sign-in provider details and account security state.
  • Business records: product names and categories, cost and selling prices, stock, stock changes, sales, expenses, notes, reports and business-profile details.
  • Images: product and business images uploaded by a seller. Private Sellbook product images are stored in an account-scoped area. Images deliberately published for a Buybook listing or shop-front are publicly accessible so buyers can view the listing.
  • Buybook activity, when enabled: shop details, public listings, subscriptions, reservation details, buyer/seller messages and related status updates.
  • Support and feedback: messages, feedback, replies and the information needed to respond to a request.
  • Historical service activity: Sellbook retains a limited set of pseudonymous visit/session keys and visit dates created while browser visit collection was active. New browser visit tracking is currently paused, so the app does not collect new visit records through that feature.
  • Device and offline data: records cached on the device and pending changes waiting to synchronise. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see local records.

Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; provide product, stock, sales, expense, report, backup and synchronisation features; operate optional Buybook listings, subscriptions, messages and reservations; answer support requests; prevent abuse; maintain service reliability; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the activity, processing may be necessary to provide the service requested by the user, comply with a legal obligation, pursue legitimate interests such as account security and service reliability, or rely on consent for an optional activity. Where consent is the basis, it may be withdrawn for future processing, subject to other lawful grounds.

Marketplace images and automated review

When a seller chooses to connect a shop to Buybook, the uploaded shop-front photograph is submitted to an automated image-classification check to determine whether it clearly depicts the outside front or entrance of a physical shop. The current classifier runs through Cloudflare Workers AI using the @cf/moondream/moondream3.1-9B-A2B model. The result may approve or reject the shop-front image; an inconclusive result does not approve the shop.

Automated image review can make mistakes. A seller who believes a photo was rejected incorrectly can contact Sellbook Support and request a review. The shop-front image may be publicly accessible when used for a connected Buybook shop. Do not upload images containing people, identity documents, private interiors or unrelated personal information.

Sellbook's current shop-front check is an image check; this notice does not describe or authorise face matching or national identity number verification.

Where information is stored and who provides services

Signed-in cloud records, authentication data and uploaded files are handled by Supabase. The configured database is hosted in the EU West (Ireland) region. The web app is hosted through Vercel and uses Cloudflare infrastructure for delivery, backup deployment and the optional shop-front image-classification service. Google may process authentication information when a user chooses Google sign-in.

These service providers process information as needed to provide their services. Depending on the service and infrastructure location, information may be processed outside Nigeria. Before unrestricted launch, the operator must review the providers' applicable data-processing terms and confirm the safeguards required for any international transfer.

Buybook listing and shop-front images are made public when the seller chooses to publish a connected shop. Public listings may be viewed by anyone. Private Sellbook product images and private business records are not intended to be publicly available.

How long information is kept

Account and business information is kept while the account is active and as needed to provide the service, respond to support requests, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. When an account is deleted, Sellbook runs its account-deletion process for cloud account data and account-owned files. Data already exported by a user, publicly copied content, and copies retained in independent provider backups may not be removed immediately by that process.

Users should export a backup and allow pending offline changes to synchronise before deleting an account. Backup files can contain sensitive business records and should be stored securely.

Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to their personal data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, users may withdraw that consent. Users may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Signed-in users can submit a privacy request through Me → Support in Sellbook and include “Privacy request” in the subject or message. The operator must publish the direct privacy contact in the operator-information section above so that people who cannot sign in can also make a request.

Security and user responsibilities

Sellbook uses authenticated access, account-scoped database permissions and server-side checks for sensitive operations. No online service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password, protect your device, sign out on shared devices, do not share your Sellbook PIN, and keep independent backups.

Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers or unrelated sensitive personal information into product names, sales notes, expense notes or support messages.

Payments and financial information

Sellbook is a business-record and inventory tool. The app does not collect or store payment-card details or hold customer funds. Users and buyers arrange payment directly with one another outside Sellbook. Sales and expense amounts entered into Sellbook are business records supplied by the user.

Updates and contact

This notice may be updated when Sellbook's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The operator should notify users of material changes through an appropriate in-app notice.

Important: the operator's legal name, place of business and direct privacy contact remain required fields. Complete them before unrestricted public launch and have this notice reviewed for the operator's actual processing activities.
