Privacy Notice

My Sales Book · Effective 23 October 2026

Please read this notice before using My Sales Book. This is a practical privacy notice for the app, not legal advice. The operator should review it for compliance with applicable Nigerian data-protection law before a public launch.

Information the app handles

  • Account information used to sign in, such as your email address and authentication identifier.
  • Business records you enter, including product names, prices, stock quantities, sales, expenses, notes, and stock adjustments.
  • Optional business profile details and product or business-logo images you choose to upload.
  • Device-local records, cached records, and pending changes used to support offline access and later synchronisation.

Where information is stored

Some information may be stored in your browser on your device. When you sign in and use cloud features, your records and uploaded images are stored using the app's configured Supabase service. Google sign-in, if used, is handled by the relevant authentication provider. Offline records may not reach the cloud until you reconnect.

Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access local records. Do not use a shared device without signing out and protecting the device.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, sales and expense recording, stock management, reports, syncing between devices, image display, backup/restore, and account deletion. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive personal information into business notes.

Sharing and service providers

Your cloud records are intended to be isolated by account. The app relies on hosting and database/authentication providers to operate. Their own terms and privacy notices may also apply. The app operator should publish the identity and contact details of the responsible data controller and any additional processors before broad public launch.

Retention, export and deletion

You can use the app's export feature to save a backup. Keep backups private and secure; exported files may contain your business records. Account deletion is intended to delete cloud account data, and may also clear local cached records for that account. Export anything you need and synchronise pending changes before deleting an account. Deletion may not remove copies already exported or held in independent backups.

Security and your choices

Use a strong, unique password, keep your device updated and locked, sign out on shared devices, and maintain backups. No online or offline storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. You should avoid storing information that is not needed for the sales-book purpose.

Contact and updates

A dedicated privacy/support contact has not been configured in this notice. The app operator should add a monitored contact channel before inviting the public. This notice may be updated as the app changes.
