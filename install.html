Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Sellbook free to install?

Yes. You can install the web app from a supported browser without downloading an APK.

How do I open the installed app?

On Android, return to your home screen or app list and tap the Sellbook icon. A normal link opened in Chrome may stay in Chrome instead of switching to the installed app. The “Open Sellbook in browser” button opens the website; use the home-screen icon to launch the installed version.

Why does Sellbook offer Google sign-in?

Google sign-in lets you access the cloud account configured for Sellbook. Use the sign-in button inside the app and complete the Google sign-in page in your browser. Do not share your password with anyone.

Will my records work offline?

Offline behaviour depends on the app version and which features have been cached or saved on the device. Do not assume cloud sign-in, cloud records or synchronisation will work without internet. Reconnect before relying on cloud features.

How do I share the installation page?

Tap “Share Sellbook” and choose WhatsApp or another app. You can also copy the installation link and send it directly.
